Approach

How an engagement
runs, step by step.

The same five steps every time, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

Steps

01

First call

about 30 minutes

We ask about your business, your customers, your numbers and what you have already tried. You ask us anything.

  • No preparation needed
  • We tell you honestly if we are not the right fit
02

Audit

start of the work

We get read access to your accounts and look at everything: ads, website, tracking, follow-up and competitors.

  • Findings ranked by how much they cost you
  • Quick fixes separated from bigger projects
03

Plan

agreed together

We set one goal, split it into workstreams and agree the budget, the channels and who does what.

  • Written plan you keep
  • Fee and scope fixed before work begins
04

Run

week by week

We set up and manage the work, or guide your team if you have people in-house.

  • Changes logged so you can see what moved
  • Anything that needs your decision is flagged
05

Review

every week, and every quarter

A short weekly call and a one-page report. Every quarter we step back and decide what to keep, change or stop.

  • One page, not a slide deck
  • Clear next steps after every review

What we need
from you.

At the start

  • Access to your ad accounts, analytics and website
  • Your rough numbers: sales, margins, average order value
  • One person on your side who can make decisions

Every week

  • About 30 minutes for the review call
  • Quick replies on anything marked as needing you
  • Honest feedback on lead or customer quality
